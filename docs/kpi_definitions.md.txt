# KPI Definitions Document

## Project: Loan Portfolio Risk Analysis

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) used within the credit risk analytics solution.

---

# 1. Total Exposure

## Definition
Total outstanding loan amount within the portfolio.

## Business Purpose
Measures the total financial exposure currently held by the institution.

## Formula

SUM(Outstanding_Amount)

## Interpretation

Higher exposure indicates greater portfolio size and increased credit risk exposure.

# 2. Loan at Risk

Definition

Total outstanding exposure associated with active, delinquent, and defaulted loans.

Business Purpose

Measures the portion of the portfolio currently exposed to repayment risk.

Formula
SUM(Outstanding_Amount)
WHERE Loan_Status IN ('Active', 'Delinquent', 'Defaulted')
Interpretation

A high loan-at-risk value may indicate elevated portfolio vulnerability.

# 3. Non-Performing Loan (NPL) Ratio

Definition

Percentage of defaulted loan exposure relative to total outstanding exposure.

Business Purpose

Measures the proportion of the portfolio classified as non-performing.

Formula
(Defaulted Exposure / Total Outstanding Exposure) * 100
Interpretation

Higher NPL ratios indicate deteriorating portfolio quality and increased default exposure.

# 4. Default Rate

Definition

Percentage of defaulted loans relative to the total active loan portfolio.

Business Purpose

Measures the frequency of loan defaults within the portfolio.

Formula
(Number of Defaulted Loans / Total Active Loans) * 100


Interpretation

An increasing default rate may indicate weakening credit quality or ineffective underwriting policies.


## 5. High Risk Rate

Definition

Percentage of total portfolio exposure classified as high risk.

Business Purpose

Measures the concentration of high-risk exposure within the portfolio.

Formula
(High Risk Exposure / Total Exposure) * 100
Interpretation

Higher high-risk rates indicate greater portfolio vulnerability.

# 6. Portfolio Risk Mix

Definition

Distribution of portfolio exposure across low-risk, medium-risk, and high-risk categories.

Business Purpose

Provides visibility into the overall risk composition of the portfolio.

Formula
(Risk Category Exposure / Total Exposure) * 100

Interpretation

A balanced portfolio should maintain controlled levels of medium-risk and high-risk exposure.


# 7. Closed Loan Ratio

Definition

Percentage of loans that have been fully repaid or closed.

Business Purpose

Measures portfolio maturity and loan repayment performance.

Formula
(Closed Loans / Total Loans) * 100
Interpretation

Higher closed loan ratios may indicate healthy repayment trends.

# 8. Risk Concentration Metrics

Definition

Measures the distribution of risk exposure across customer and loan segments.

Segmentation Areas
Location
Loan Type
Employment Type
Age Group
Business Purpose

Identifies segments contributing most significantly to portfolio risk.

Interpretation

High concentration within specific segments may require targeted risk mitigation strategies.
