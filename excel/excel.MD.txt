## Excel Analytical Models

The project was first explored and validated in Excel using pivot-based analytical models before the data was migrated into SQL and Power BI for enterprise reporting. 

The Excel workbook contains supporting portfolio risk models used for:
- loan distribution analysis,
- exposure segmentation,
- risk concentration monitoring,
- and portfolio validation prior to dashboard development.

The workbook includes:
- Loan Count Distribution Matrix
- Outstanding Exposure Matrix
- Portfolio Risk Percentage Distribution
- Pivot-based Risk Segmentation Models
